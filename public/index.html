Psychometrics · AI in Assessment · Evaluation

Gözde Sırgancı, PhD

Measurement scientist building fair, valid assessments with modern psychometrics and AI

I have spent more than ten years designing, calibrating and validating tests, from national computerized adaptive exams to test items generated with large language models. My work sits where item response theory meets machine learning and large language models, and it always comes back to one question: does this score mean the same thing for everyone who takes the test?

Selected work

Projects
LLMs · AIG · RCT

Large language models in assessment and learning

Ongoing research on using LLMs to generate test items, and on the evidence needed to show that generated items are valid, fair and ready for operational use. In a randomized controlled trial, I also studied individual and peer-supported ChatGPT use in nursing case analysis and its effects on diagnostic skills and cognitive load, published in Nurse Education in Practice (2026).

Read the RCT article →

SMU · Language

Language and literacy assessment

Psychometric work on measures of children's language skills: oral reading fluency research shared in several publications and NCME presentations, and partial credit model calibration, scoring and validity evidence for the MELVA-S science vocabulary assessment.

DIF · Invariance

Fairness and DIF research

Work on whether tests treat every group the same: DIF detection methods, measurement invariance across many countries in PISA, mixture Rasch models, and a new study testing whether generative AI can flag biased items.

Explore the research and publications →

Scales · Adaptation

Scale development and adaptation

Developing new measurement instruments and adapting existing ones to Turkish culture with colleagues in health, sport and education, from esports orientation and children's digital addiction to rational drug use. Each study builds evidence on factor structure, reliability and measurement invariance.
